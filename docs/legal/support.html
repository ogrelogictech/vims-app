Common questions

How do I add inspectors to my company? In the app, open the ☰ menu → Inspectors, share your company code, and have each inspector tap "Join a company with a code" when they sign up.

Where are the admin settings? Company admins find Manage checklist, Plans & pricing and Inspectors in the ☰ menu and under Settings → Admin.

How do I delete an inspection? On the Home screen, tap ⋯ next to the inspection → Delete inspection. Some states require reports to be kept for 3–5 years, so save the report first.

How do I cancel my subscription? Settings → Plan & billing → Cancel subscription.

How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete account. To request deletion of your data, email us.
