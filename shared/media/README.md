`vims-splash.mp4`: the launch splash video. 6.6 seconds, 720×1280 H.264, about 5.7 MB. It is a compressed copy of the updated splash video supplied with the team's iOS review on October 12, 2026 (1080×1920, 50 fps, 15.2 MB): white satin background with "V.I.M.S. — Vision Inspection Management Solutions", spelled correctly and fully in frame. It replaces the October 6 `VIMS - Splash Video 1.mp4`.

It plays once on cold launch: muted, full screen, fitted inside the screen, with the bands above and below colored from the video's own edges. Tapping skips it. Afterwards the app goes to Sign in, or to Home if a session is saved. With Reduce Motion or animations off, the last frame shows for about 1 second instead. The apps' safety timeout (8 s) is longer than the video.
