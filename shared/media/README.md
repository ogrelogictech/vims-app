`vims-splash.mp4`: the launch splash video. 5 seconds, 720×1280 H.264, about 5.7 MB. It is a compressed copy of the client-supplied `VIMS - Splash Video 1.mp4` (1080×1920, 14.6 MB, received October 6, 2026), which corrects the earlier "Insjection" typo.

It plays once on cold launch: muted, full screen, fitted inside the screen, with the bands above and below colored from the video's own edges. Tapping skips it. Afterwards the app goes to Sign in, or to Home if a session is saved. With Reduce Motion or animations off, the last frame shows for about 1 second instead.

Around the 3-second mark the video's own zoom briefly pushes the tagline past the frame edges. That is in the source video, not caused by the app.
