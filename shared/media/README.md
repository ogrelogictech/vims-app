Launch splash videos (team, Oct 14, 2026; "V.I.M.S." with "Vision Inspection / Management Solutions" stacked under it, fully in frame). 10 seconds, H.264, 24 fps, about 1.6 MB each.
- `vims-splash.mp4`: 720×1280 portrait, used when the screen is taller than wide (phones, tablets in portrait).
- `vims-splash-wide.mp4`: 1280×720 landscape (the team's iPad version), used when the screen is wider than tall (tablets/iPads in landscape).

Plays once on cold launch: muted, fitted inside the screen, bands colored from the video's edges. Tap skips it. The safety timeout is the video's own length plus a margin, so it always plays to the end. With Reduce Motion or animations off, the last frame shows for about 1 second instead.
